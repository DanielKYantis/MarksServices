# Content Style Guide

## Voice

- Local
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Mature-homeowner friendly
- Technically credible
- Not flashy or hype-driven

## Good Copy Patterns

- “serving homeowners in Sun City, Berry Creek, and Georgetown”
- “client-location service”
- “home repairs, maintenance, and installations”
- “plumbing fixtures, water treatment, light electrical fixtures, doors, locks, drywall, trim, cabinets, grab bars, punch lists, and handyman services”
- “confirm HOA, permit, utility, and manufacturer requirements when applicable”

## Avoid

- “Best in Texas”
- “Guaranteed same-day”
- “24/7 emergency” unless verified
- “HOA approved”
- “licensed for everything”
- “visit our office”
- “showroom”
- “full-service construction company”
- “commercial and industrial construction”
- generic lorem ipsum or template language

## Local Trust Notes

Use location terms naturally:

- Sun City, Georgetown, TX 78633
- Berry Creek, Georgetown, TX 78628
- Georgetown, TX 78626 / 78627
- Williamson County

Do not overuse every location phrase in every paragraph.

## Service Boundary Notes

Where service overlaps excluded categories, keep scope narrow:

- Small carpentry repairs are acceptable if not framed as full remodeling.
- Fixture replacement is acceptable if not framed as full electrical rebuilds.
- Water heater/water treatment content is acceptable if technically accurate and properly licensed/coordinated when required.
- Drywall repair and paint touch-ups are acceptable; do not market full painting contractor services.
- Minor exterior maintenance is acceptable; do not market roofing, deck building, fence building, or large exterior construction.
- AC vent/register cover repair is acceptable; do not market HVAC equipment service.
- Exterior-visible work should include HOA/permit caution when appropriate.
